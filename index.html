How to play

  • Collect every pearl in the maze. Some sit in clams; others are buried in sand, so drill through where it glints.
  • When the last pearl is collected the exit hatch opens. Swim out before your air runs out.
  • Stop on a vent to refill your air. Currents carry you along, or hold you back.
  • Ping stuns every enemy near you for a few seconds. Ram a stunned one for 200, 400, 800, then 1,600 points.
  • The eel follows your wake, the crab charges along its channel after raising its claws, and the puffer blinks before it puffs into spikes.

Controls

  • Touch: touch and slide anywhere on the left third of the screen to steer; tap PING on the right.
  • Keyboard: arrows or WASD to swim, Space, Z or Return to ping, Esc or P to pause, M for sound.
  • Game controller: D-pad or left stick to swim, A to ping, Menu to pause.

In the pause menu, Corner cutting and Diagonal turns make tight turns easier, and View switches between the Arcade, Wide and Cabinet screens.

Common questions

I keep missing narrow turns.
Push toward the turn a little before you reach it: the sub remembers it and turns at the opening. Corner cutting (on by default) lets it turn a moment early; Diagonal turns lets you lean the stick toward a turn while you keep swimming.

There's no sound.
Check the speaker button in the game and the silent switch on your iPhone; the game follows it.
