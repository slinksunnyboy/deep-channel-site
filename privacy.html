Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Deep Channel is a single-player arcade game for iPhone, iPad and Mac. It is built to work entirely on your device. This policy explains what that means for your information.

Information we collect

None. Deep Channel does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal information. In particular, the game:

  • does not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number or any account details;
  • contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code, and does not track you across apps or websites;
  • does not access your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone;
  • does not send gameplay data to us or to anyone else, and makes no network requests of its own.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your high score and settings such as sound on or off, the turn helpers and the screen view. This information is stored only on your device using the standard system preferences, and is removed if you delete the app.

Purchases

Deep Channel has no in-app purchases and no advertising.

Children

Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
